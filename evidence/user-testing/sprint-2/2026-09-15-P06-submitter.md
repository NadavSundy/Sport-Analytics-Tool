## Participant comments

The participant's overall feedback was that the only notable issues were needing the correct competition scope and being restricted to selecting existing fixtures. Everything else was easy to use.

Additional comments made during tasks:

- Existing competition access should be visible in the same area used to request additional access.
- Large dropdowns should support search/filtering, especially competition-access requests and fixture selection.
- A clear workflow should exist for submitting a completely new fixture rather than only selecting an existing fixture.
- Single fixture, Season, and Back catalogue were clearly distinguished.
- The successful season-upload message was clear and made sense.
- Rejection feedback clearly identified the error that needed correction.
- The full report/export was easy to find.
