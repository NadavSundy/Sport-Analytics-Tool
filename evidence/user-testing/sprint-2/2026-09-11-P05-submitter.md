## Findings and decisions

### P05-F01 — Season versus batch terminology is unclear

- **Related task:** BAT-01
- **Severity:** S3 — Medium
- **Decision:** Accept
- **Reason:** The participant could not form a clear mental model of the difference between a season, a season upload and a batch. The interface should describe the user's goal in plain language and keep implementation terminology secondary.
- **Linked issue:** #498
- **Implementation status:** Implemented before the P06 session.
- **Retest:** **Verified improved by P06.** P06 reported that Single fixture, Season and Back catalogue were clearly differentiated and easy to find.

### P05-F02 — Validation errors are too technical and code-like

- **Related tasks:** SUB-03, SUB-04
- **Severity:** S2 — High
- **Decision:** Accept
- **Reason:** Understanding a validation failure and knowing how to recover are core submitter tasks. The original feedback did not provide sufficiently plain-language, actionable guidance.
- **Linked issue:** #499
- **Implementation status:** Implemented before the P06 session.
- **Retest:** **Partially verified by P06.** P06 reported that the tested rejection clearly showed the error and what needed correction. The exact invalid single-fixture path was not retested because P06's single-fixture task was blocked earlier in the workflow.

### P05-F03 — Guided submission exposes or requires internal database IDs

- **Related task:** SUB-02
- **Severity:** S2 — High
- **Decision:** Accept
- **Reason:** A normal submitter should not need internal database identifiers. Guided submission should resolve readable fixture/team/player/competition context to internal identifiers.
- **Linked issue:** #500
- **Implementation status:** Implemented before the P06 session.
- **Retest:** **Not verified by P06.** P06 could not exercise a new single-fixture upload because the workflow only allowed selection of an existing fixture.

### P05-F04 — Approved submitter cannot request additional competition scope

- **Related task:** AUTH-02
- **Severity:** S2 — High
- **Decision:** Accept
- **Reason:** Competition scope is required for ordinary submission work. A submitter who needs another competition must have a visible request path rather than reaching an undocumented dead end.
- **Linked issues:** #501, #519
- **Implementation status:** Implemented before the P06 session.
- **Retest:** **Verified improved by P06.** P06 found the additional-competition request easily. P06 separately suggested showing current scopes in the same area and adding search to large selectors.

### P05-F05 — Submission page does not clearly explain what happened or what comes next

- **Related tasks:** SUB-01, SUB-02, BAT-01, BAT-03
- **Severity:** S3 — Medium
- **Decision:** Accept
- **Reason:** The participant reported that the submission area did not clearly communicate receipt, processing state or the next action.
- **Linked issues:** #498, #520
- **Implementation status:** Implemented before the P06 session.
- **Retest:** **Verified improved by P06 for the batch/season path.** P06 said the season-upload success message was clear and made sense, and later found the submission status without assistance.
