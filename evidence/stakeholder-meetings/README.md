# Stakeholder meetings

Records of meetings at which the product was demonstrated to, or reviewed with, the project
stakeholder, together with the disposition of every item of feedback raised.

Sprint-bounded stakeholder records remain under `evidence/sprints/<sprint>/`, where they sit beside
the planning, stand-up and close-out evidence for the same sprint. This directory holds stakeholder
meetings that are tied to a feature or a milestone rather than to a sprint ceremony, so that a
demonstration of one feature can be traced from the feedback to the work it caused without reading a
whole sprint folder.

## What a record must contain

- The date, and what was demonstrated or reviewed.
- Who attended, and whether the meeting was recorded or minuted at the time. A record written up
  afterwards must say so.
- Each item of feedback, stated as the stakeholder raised it rather than as the team would prefer to
  read it.
- **The disposition of every item**: the issue it was converted into, the issue it updated, or a
  documented decision to make no change with the reason. An item with no disposition is not finished
  evidence.

## Integrity

The rules in [`evidence/README.md`](../README.md) apply in full. In particular: do not invent
meetings, attendance, decisions, results or feedback. Where a detail was not retained at the time,
record that it was not retained rather than reconstructing it.
